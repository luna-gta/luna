## 📖 The Story of Luna — Why It Shut Down

Luna was originally a **paid** mod menu sold by the Luna Cheats team, with resellers taking cryptocurrency payments. It became one of the most popular GTA Online menus thanks to its protections and recovery features. Then Take-Two Interactive — the parent company of Rockstar Games — took legal action against the cheat makers. Luna Cheats replaced its website with a short statement saying that after discussions with Take-Two Interactive, it was immediately ceasing all maintenance, development and distribution of its cheat menu services. Reports at the time said the team even gave the money it had made to charity.

That is why Luna is listed as **Offline / discontinued** today, and why there is no official Luna website anymore. The free **Luna Mod Menu** release from 2023 is what still circulates — and this page is built around that free release, not the old paid menu.

## 🔧 Troubleshooting Common Issues

- ❌ **Menu Does Not Open**
  Script Hook V or ScriptHookV.NET is probably missing or outdated.
  **Solution:** Install the latest Script Hook V, ASI Loader and ScriptHookV.NET, then try F5 (or F4 on some builds) again in Story Mode.

- ❌ **Windows Defender Deletes the File**
  Defender flags almost every mod menu as a threat.
  **Solution:** Add the Luna folder to Windows Defender exclusions, then re-extract the file.

- ❌ **Game Crashes When the Menu Starts**
  Usually caused by a conflicting mod or a GTA V update the menu does not support.
  **Solution:** Remove other ASI mods temporarily, verify your GTA V files, and remember Luna's free release is from 2023 — it may not support the newest GTA V patches.

- ❌ **Numpad Keys Do Nothing**
  Laptops and compact keyboards often have no numpad.
  **Solution:** Press `Ctrl + Windows + O` to open the Windows on-screen keyboard, which has a numpad you can click.

- ❌ **"Is Luna still working?"**
  The original Luna service is shut down and no longer updated. The free release may work on older GTA V builds but is not guaranteed on the latest patches.
  **Solution:** If Luna does not work on your game version, try a maintained menu instead — Kiddion's Modest Menu and Menyoo are both actively used and free.
